# ORION — DEFINITIVE PHASE 1 HERO REDESIGN
## Build an immersive 3D cyber-intelligence experience
## Inspired by the storytelling quality of our VEILLE landing page,
## but DO NOT copy VEILLE's design, assets, content, or layout.

============================================================
0. CONTEXT
============================================================

You are working inside the existing ORION repository.

ORION is:

AI-POWERED CYBER THREAT INTELLIGENCE & DIGITAL TRUST PLATFORM

Tagline:

FROM DIGITAL SIGNALS TO ACTIONABLE THREATS.

The current landing page has already gone through several iterations.

The latest iteration is NOT acceptable.

It currently looks like:

- a normal SaaS hero
- with a giant glowing cyan sphere
- with text beside it
- with decorative network effects

That is NOT the target.

We need to redesign the HERO EXPERIENCE around a cinematic,
interactive 3D intelligence environment.

The inspiration is the QUALITY OF EXPERIENCE we previously achieved
with the VEILLE landing page:

- cinematic 3D storytelling
- environment-driven information
- spatial data visualization
- progressive discovery
- scroll-controlled transformation
- restrained glass UI
- premium dark visual language
- strong negative space
- the 3D visualization actually explains the product

However:

DO NOT COPY VEILLE.

Do not copy:
- its exact globe
- its exact UI
- its exact colors
- its exact animations
- its exact layout
- its exact content
- its exact visual objects

ORION must have its OWN visual identity.

============================================================
1. CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
============================================================

VEILLE's philosophy was:

THE VISUALIZATION IS PART OF THE EXPLANATION.

ORION should take that further:

THE THREAT NETWORK IS THE EXPLANATION.

The user should understand ORION by watching the environment transform.

The experience should communicate:

DIGITAL SIGNAL
      ↓
DETECTION
      ↓
EVIDENCE
      ↓
RISK
      ↓
RELATIONSHIP
      ↓
EXPLANATION
      ↓
RESPONSE

without requiring the user to read a wall of text.

The page should feel like:

"I am entering an intelligence system."

Not:

"I am viewing a website with a 3D background."

============================================================
2. DO NOT START WITH THE GLOBE
============================================================

The biggest mistake in the previous implementation was treating:

"3D globe"

as the feature.

The feature is:

"progressive cyber-intelligence visualization."

The globe is only the initial environment.

The real subject is the intelligence emerging around it.

The visual story is:

QUIET DIGITAL WORLD
        ↓
SIGNALS
        ↓
SIGNAL RELATIONSHIPS
        ↓
THREAT NETWORK
        ↓
EVIDENCE
        ↓
RISK
        ↓
ACTION

============================================================
3. INITIAL HERO STATE
============================================================

At page load, the scene should be calm.

Background:

- near-black
- extremely subtle navy atmosphere
- very sparse particles
- subtle depth
- no giant gradients
- no excessive neon

A dark technical Earth is visible on the RIGHT.

The Earth should NOT be bright cyan.

The Earth should be:

- dark navy/black
- recognizable
- subtly illuminated
- atmospheric
- technical
- sophisticated

Only a few small points of light should be visible.

Think:

A security analyst has just opened ORION.

The system is observing.

Nothing is screaming for attention yet.

============================================================
4. EARTH
============================================================

Create a convincing stylized Earth.

It must be immediately recognizable as Earth.

Required:

- visible continent silhouettes
- dark surface
- subtle topographic/geographic detail
- subtle latitude/longitude or technical grid
- atmospheric rim
- very restrained blue/cyan illumination
- dark oceans
- subtle city/signal lights if useful

DO NOT create:

- solid cyan sphere
- glowing energy ball
- wireframe-only planet
- giant neon planet
- gaming-style planet
- excessive bloom

The Earth should be visually sophisticated enough that a screenshot
could plausibly belong to a professional cyber-intelligence platform.

Photorealism is NOT required.

Recognition + atmosphere + technical detail are more important.

============================================================
5. HERO COMPOSITION
============================================================

Desktop:

LEFT:
ORION branding/content

RIGHT:
3D Earth + intelligence environment

Maintain strong negative space.

The globe must NEVER overlap:

- ORION title
- tagline
- description
- ENTER ORION
- EXPLORE INTELLIGENCE

The composition should feel deliberately designed.

Do not make the Earth so large that it becomes the only thing visible.

Do not make it so small that it becomes decorative.

Target:

Earth occupies approximately 40–50% of the visual hero area.

============================================================
6. ORION HERO COPY
============================================================

Keep the current strong hierarchy.

Eyebrow:

AI-POWERED CYBER THREAT INTELLIGENCE

Title:

ORION

Tagline:

From Digital Signals
to Actionable Threats.

Supporting description:

ORION unifies digital signals across phishing, websites, identity,
media and authentication into explainable threat intelligence.

Primary:

ENTER ORION

Secondary:

EXPLORE INTELLIGENCE

Do not make the copy overly long.

The 3D scene should carry much of the explanation.

============================================================
7. STAGE ONE — OBSERVE
============================================================

Initial state.

The Earth is distant.

Only 3–5 signal points exist.

Each point is:

- tiny
- white/cyan
- soft halo
- geographically positioned

No huge HUD cards.

No giant network.

No visual chaos.

The environment should feel alive but quiet.

A subtle label may appear:

DIGITAL SIGNALS

or:

OBSERVING THREAT SURFACE

============================================================
8. SCROLL — ENTER THE WORLD
============================================================

Scrolling should NOT simply move the webpage past a static globe.

The hero should behave like an immersive scene.

As the user scrolls:

THE CAMERA MOVES.

The user feels like they are approaching the intelligence environment.

Camera movement:

- slow
- smooth
- controlled
- slightly cinematic
- subtle parallax
- no nausea
- no sudden jumps

The Earth gradually becomes larger.

The user is effectively entering ORION's view of the digital world.

============================================================
9. STAGE TWO — DETECT
============================================================

As the camera approaches:

One signal activates.

It emits a subtle pulse.

A small HUD card appears.

Example:

PHISHING

Suspicious Communication

Then another signal:

WEBSITE

Malicious Domain

The cards should feel spatially attached to the 3D scene.

They are NOT ordinary website cards.

They should feel like intelligence annotations.

Use:

- dark translucent glass
- very thin borders
- restrained cyan
- white typography
- subtle blur
- small technical icon

Do not create giant cards.

============================================================
10. STAGE THREE — CONNECT
============================================================

Now something important happens.

The PHISHING signal connects to WEBSITE.

The connection should:

1. appear gradually
2. illuminate
3. carry a tiny travelling signal particle
4. settle into a subtle persistent line

The user visually understands:

These are not independent events.

They are related.

The experience should feel almost investigative.

============================================================
11. STAGE FOUR — CORRELATE
============================================================

Continue scrolling.

Activate:

IDENTITY

Then:

AUTHENTICATION

Then optionally:

MEDIA

and:

TECHNICAL

The network progressively forms.

Use deliberate relationships.

For example:

PHISHING
    │
    ▼
WEBSITE
    │
    ▼
IDENTITY
    │
    ▼
AUTHENTICATION

And:

MEDIA → IDENTITY

TECHNICAL → AUTHENTICATION

Do NOT randomly connect everything.

DO NOT use Math.random() to generate relationships.

Every connection should have a conceptual purpose.

============================================================
12. GEOGRAPHIC INTELLIGENCE
============================================================

The signal points should be positioned ON the Earth.

Use a small number of illustrative locations.

Examples:

- North America
- Europe
- India / South Asia
- East Asia
- Southeast Asia

These are visualization points.

They are NOT claims about real attacks.

Do not display fake real-time attack statistics.

Do not imply that ORION is currently monitoring the entire world.

The globe is a representation of the digital threat surface.

============================================================
13. THE EARTH SHOULD EVENTUALLY BECOME SECONDARY
============================================================

This is extremely important.

At the beginning:

EARTH > NETWORK

As the user scrolls:

EARTH = NETWORK

Later:

NETWORK > EARTH

Eventually:

The Earth becomes a subtle background/context.

The intelligence relationships become the primary visual.

This creates the feeling that the user has moved:

FROM THE WORLD
INTO THE SIGNALS
INTO THE RELATIONSHIPS

============================================================
14. STAGE FIVE — EVIDENCE
============================================================

Once the network is established, introduce evidence.

A small intelligence panel can appear.

Example:

THREAT SIGNAL

PHISHING

Evidence:

• suspicious URL structure
• impersonated identity
• credential-oriented behavior

Keep it visually minimal.

Do not create a giant dashboard.

This is a cinematic preview of ORION's explainability.

============================================================
15. STAGE SIX — RISK
============================================================

The connected network should then produce a subtle risk visualization.

Example:

RISK ASSESSMENT

HIGH

Risk Score
78

The number should be clearly presented as:

ILLUSTRATIVE

or otherwise avoid implying live backend data.

The point is to visually communicate:

SIGNALS → EVIDENCE → RISK

============================================================
16. STAGE SEVEN — RESPONSE
============================================================

At the end of the hero sequence, the intelligence network should
resolve into a clean conceptual flow:

SIGNAL
    ↓
EVIDENCE
    ↓
RISK
    ↓
RELATIONSHIP
    ↓
RESPONSE

This should become the visual bridge into the next landing section.

============================================================
17. DO NOT KEEP THE 3D SCENE BEHIND THE ENTIRE WEBSITE
============================================================

The current implementation mounts OrionGlobeScene as:

fixed inset-0

behind the entire landing page.

Do NOT continue this architecture if it causes the 3D scene to remain
a generic background throughout the entire page.

The 3D hero experience should be intentionally scoped to the hero
story.

The scene can be sticky during the hero's scroll narrative.

Once the hero story completes:

transition naturally into normal HTML/React landing sections.

The 3D scene should have a clear beginning, evolution, and conclusion.

============================================================
18. NETWORK VISUAL LANGUAGE
============================================================

Connections should be:

- thin
- elegant
- slightly curved
- luminous but restrained
- dark enough to preserve hierarchy

Use white for important signal events.

Use cyan/blue for intelligence relationships.

Avoid:

- thick neon beams
- laser effects
- giant glowing tubes
- excessive particles

The network should feel analytical, not magical.

============================================================
19. WHITE SIGNALS ARE IMPORTANT
============================================================

Use white or near-white points as the highest-contrast signal events.

This is intentional.

Visual hierarchy:

WHITE
= active signal / important event

CYAN
= intelligence / connection

DARK BLUE
= environment

WHITE TEXT
= primary information

This should give the scene a sophisticated security-platform appearance.

============================================================
20. ATMOSPHERE
============================================================

Use depth carefully.

Possible:

- subtle fog
- atmospheric scattering
- soft rim light
- very light volumetric effect
- sparse particles
- subtle depth-of-field if performance permits

Do not overdo post-processing.

The scene should remain crisp.

============================================================
21. MOUSE INTERACTION
============================================================

Add subtle cursor interaction.

Examples:

- Earth rotates slightly in response
- camera shifts by a few pixels
- signal points have tiny parallax
- nearby HUD annotation reacts subtly

Do NOT make the entire scene follow the mouse aggressively.

============================================================
22. SCROLL + MOUSE MUST WORK TOGETHER
============================================================

Scroll controls:

THE STORY.

Mouse controls:

THE DEPTH.

They should not fight each other.

Scroll determines:

- camera distance
- network reveal
- signal activation
- connection activation
- Earth/network transition

Mouse determines:

- subtle parallax
- small perspective adjustment

============================================================
23. VISUAL STORY SHOULD WORK WITHOUT TEXT
============================================================

This is a major acceptance criterion.

Imagine temporarily removing all HTML copy.

The 3D scene should still communicate:

WORLD
→ SIGNALS
→ CONNECTIONS
→ NETWORK
→ THREAT
→ INTELLIGENCE

If the answer is no, the visualization needs improvement.

============================================================
24. HUD DISCIPLINE
============================================================

Use fewer HUD elements than the current implementation.

Do NOT surround the globe with:

10+ cards
status panels
random labels
random counters

At any one moment:

approximately 1–3 important annotations.

They should appear because something happened in the scene.

Example:

signal activates
→ annotation appears.

connection forms
→ relationship label appears.

risk emerges
→ risk panel appears.

The UI should feel reactive to the intelligence.

============================================================
25. CURRENT STATUS BAR
============================================================

The current:

SIGNALS
INTELLIGENCE
CORRELATION
RISK ENGINE

status strip can remain, but make it much more subtle.

It should NOT compete with:

ORION
Earth
network

Use it as a small system-state element.

============================================================
26. PERFORMANCE
============================================================

The experience must remain smooth on an ordinary laptop.

Use:

- one primary WebGL canvas
- efficient geometry
- limited particles
- moderate Earth geometry
- optimized materials
- deterministic node data
- efficient animation loop
- minimal React re-renders

Avoid:

- thousands of animated meshes
- enormous textures
- expensive post-processing chains
- unnecessary physics
- multiple WebGL canvases

The visual quality should come from:

composition
lighting
depth
animation
information design

NOT brute-force GPU usage.

============================================================
27. RESPONSIVE EXPERIENCE
============================================================

Desktop is the cinematic showcase.

Tablet:

- reduce globe size
- reduce node count
- reduce HUD
- simplify network

Mobile:

- preserve the story
- simplify the 3D scene substantially
- keep text readable
- maintain CTA usability
- prevent horizontal overflow

If WebGL is unavailable:

provide a graceful static fallback.

============================================================
28. REDUCED MOTION
============================================================

Respect:

prefers-reduced-motion

Reduced motion should:

- disable major camera travel
- reduce globe rotation
- disable travelling particles
- simplify transitions

But preserve:

- Earth
- signals
- relationships
- content
- CTA

============================================================
30. DETERMINISTIC DATA
============================================================

Do NOT use Math.random() for:

- network relationships
- signal locations
- threat topology
- animation stages

Use explicit deterministic configuration.

For example:

const intelligenceGraph = {
  nodes: [...],
  relationships: [...]
}

This makes the experience reproducible and maintainable.
