3. The biggest problem I still see: process_mocked_analysis()

This has not been fixed.

Your latest orchestrator.py still contains:

def process_mocked_analysis(...)

and:

"""
Process a mocked analysis...
"""

Then every evidence object is still constructed as:

category="system"
value="mocked"
source_engine="mocked_detector"
description=f"Mocked evidence for {et}"

This is now more problematic than before because your actual detectors are real.

You currently have:

REAL ML
   ↓
phishing engine
   ↓
p_model + evidence
   ↓
process_mocked_analysis()
   ↓
mocked_detector
   ↓
canonical incident

That is architecturally ugly.

This should be your next fix.

Rename/rework this into something like:

process_analysis()

and introduce a proper normalized detector result:

DetectorResult
├── evidence
├── p_model
├── ml_insight
├── missing_ratio
├── entities
├── artifacts
└── model_metadata

Then:

Detector
   ↓
DetectorResult
   ↓
Orchestrator
   ↓
Risk Engine
   ↓
Canonical Incident

This would make ORION's architecture substantially cleaner.
