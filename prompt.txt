# 17. RISK ENGINE — DO NOT BREAK IT

The v3.3 risk engine is a core differentiator.

Preserve:

```text
E = 1 - Π(1 - wi)

P = model probability
OR
P = 0.9E in rules-only mode

C = clamp(1 - D - 0.5M, 0.2, 1.0)

F = 0.5E + 0.5C

Raw = 100 × P × F × Impact
```

Then apply:

* clamp
* policy floor
* cap
* inconclusive logic
* severity band
* half-up rounding

Do not move risk logic into detectors.

Do not let frontend calculate risk.

All risk must originate from the backend risk engine.
